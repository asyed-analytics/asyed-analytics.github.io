01 / About

Analytical mind,
operational hands.

I'm a Business Analytics graduate who is just as comfortable interrogating a dataset as I am running the floor of a fast-paced operation. That combination — the analytical rigour of an MSc Distinction and the practical instinct of hands-on team leadership — is what I bring to every problem.

My work sits where data meets decisions: building freight cost models, retail performance dashboards and operational reports that don't just look good, but actually change what a business does next.

I work primarily in Advanced Excel and Power BI, grounding every project in real industry benchmarks and documenting every assumption — because analysis you can't explain is analysis you can't trust.
